      Methods

      How products were found, what counts as an AI echo product, and what each verification level means. Maintained for the ImageGuideEcho Registry.

      Inclusion

      1. The clearance appears on FDA’s Artificial Intelligence-Enabled Medical Devices list, or was found through openFDA under an AI-specific product code (QJU, QIH, QVD, QUO, SDJ, POK) with a cardiac ultrasound indication.
      2. The device input is ultrasound of the heart: transthoracic, point-of-care or handheld, fetal echocardiography, or procedural echo.
      3. Standalone software and ultrasound systems both qualify. A system clearance is included only when its FDA summary names a cardiac AI feature. System clearances whose AI feature is non-cardiac, or not identifiable in the summary, are listed under exclusions.

      Exclusion

      • ECG-based estimators of echocardiographic phenotypes (low ejection fraction, cardiac amyloidosis, structural heart disease). The input is an ECG, not an echo.
      • Digital stethoscope and phonocardiography software.
      • Cardiac CT and cardiac MR analysis software.
      • Ultrasound AI for other organs (bladder, lung, nerve, liver, breast, obstetric biometry).

      Sources and pipeline

      1. Inventory. FDA AI-enabled device list (CSV) and openFDA 510(k) endpoint queries by product code, applicant, and device name. openFDA is authoritative for decision dates, product codes, and applicant names.
      2. FDA summaries. The 510(k) summary or De Novo decision summary PDF for every clearance was downloaded from accessdata.fda.gov and text-extracted. Indications, predicates, changes, performance testing, dataset descriptions, and flags were read from those documents.
      3. Publications. PubMed and Crossref lookups for each product; a paper is shown as resolved only when its DOI or PMID resolved during extraction and its title matched.
      4. Verification. A second, adversarial pass re-read every quoted performance claim against the source text, re-checked dates and codes against openFDA, and re-resolved every DOI. Unsupported claims were removed.
      5. Build. scripts/build-data.mjs merges the verified research files with openFDA and writes data/products.js.

      Build

        Update procedure

        1. node scripts/refresh-openfda.mjs re-pulls openFDA records for every known submission and prints new AI-coded cardiac ultrasound clearances to review.
        2. Add new families to data/research/families.json, research them, and drop the verified JSON into data/research/.
        3. node scripts/build-data.mjs then node scripts/gen-registry.mjs.
        4. node scripts/validate.cjs renders the site headless, checks for console errors, and refreshes the screenshots.
